## Use notes

- The ownership, parcel area, zoning label, building area, and construction year come from public tax/GIS records and are suitable for preliminary presentation work, not legal due diligence.
- The parking count is an estimate and should retain the tilde or an “approx.” label.
- “Early-2000s / established” is an objective vintage description. A subjective condition label such as “dated” should be based on a current property inspection.
- Reconfirm zoning conditions, overlays, title matters, and incentive eligibility before making an acquisition, development, or financing decision.
