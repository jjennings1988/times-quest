## CD5117 use limitations

The controlling zoning-case document states that the district allows all uses permitted in PI except:

1. All agricultural uses.
2. All residential uses.
3. Cemeteries.
4. Minor utilities.
5. TV/HDTV/AM/FM broadcast facilities.
6. Riding stables.
7. Golf courses, driving ranges and country clubs.
8. Overnight accommodations.
9. All personal and professional services.
10. All light-industrial uses.

## Development interpretation

- The existing YMCA can continue as an established lawful use. The current ordinance's distinction between a club/lodge and a physical fitness center should be resolved with Greensboro Planning before designing a significant addition or change of use.
- The site directly adjoins residential zoning along part of its perimeter, so the 35-foot side/rear setback and the residential-adjacency height rule will affect at least part of the development envelope.
- The approximate parking-only expansion screen is: 275 estimated spaces × 300 SF per space = 82,500 SF supported GFA; less 74,348 SF existing = approximately 8,152 SF. Do not present this as approved or vested capacity.
- The Airport Overlay does not prohibit the existing institutional use, but it adds airport-review documentation at the 50-foot height threshold.
- Any development potential should be tested through a current boundary/topographic survey, zoning determination, concept site plan and Technical Review Committee consultation.
